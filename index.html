How It Works

1

Deposit

Tenant pays deposit using simulated UPI or card payment via the SecureNest platform.

2

Escrow Account

Platform creates a smart escrow account and verifies KYC for both landlord and tenant.

3

Investment

Funds are automatically invested into a secure liquid mutual fund, generating returns.

4

Settlement

At lease completion, the landlord triggers the release in their dashboard and funds return to the tenant's primary bank account with accrued interest — automatically, no hassle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the deposit invested?

Standard deposits stay idle. We use liquid mutual funds to safely generate returns on that "dead capital" without compromising liquidity.

Is the landlord's money secure?

Absolutely. The money is lien-marked in the landlord's favor. They have a guaranteed claim on the principal amount, much like a bank guarantee.

How does the tenant get the money back?

Once the lease ends and the landlord triggers the release in their dashboard, funds move back to the tenant's primary bank account with interest.

About SecureNest

Our Mission

SecureNest was founded on the belief that rental security deposits shouldn't be "dead capital." In India alone, billions of rupees are locked away in non-interest-earning accounts. We bridge the gap between tenant liquidity and landlord security through smart financial technology.

Our platform ensures that every rupee of a security deposit is working, earning interest for the tenant while providing a lien-marked, bank-grade guarantee for the property owner.
